# Building geography provenance

City and County of San Francisco, Department of Technology, **Building Footprints**, **ynuv-fyni**. Raw GeoJSON, original metadata and attached method PDF are retained in `source/`.

- Catalog: https://data.sfgov.org/Geographic-Locations-and-Boundaries/Building-Footprints/ynuv-fyni
- Metadata: https://data.sfgov.org/api/views/ynuv-fyni.json
- PDF: https://data.sfgov.org/api/views/ynuv-fyni/files/e6e73cd0-15be-47a8-9463-9c9a725b72c3?download=true&filename=SF_BldgFoot_2017-05_description.pdf
- Method prepared 2017-05-01; Pictometry **2010** footprints and airborne LiDAR. Later parcel-guided splitting is not a new survey. 50 cm feature-height surface subtracts bare earth from first return; may include roof/tree effects. Median is a summary, not a detailed roof.
- Published license: **Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL) 1.0**, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1.0/ . Confirmed in metadata and PDF. Repository MIT does not override data licenses.
- Opening-block query (`source/buildings.geojson`): `https://data.sfgov.org/resource/ynuv-fyni.geojson?$where=intersects(shape,'POLYGON((-122.4118 37.7825,-122.4085 37.7825,-122.4085 37.7842,-122.4118 37.7842,-122.4118 37.7825))')&$limit=1000&$order=sf16_bldgid` (URL-encode query) — **58 records** around Turk/Taylor/Mason.
- Wider Tenderloin corridor query (`source/buildings_tenderloin_west.geojson`): `https://data.sfgov.org/resource/ynuv-fyni.geojson?$where=intersects(shape,'POLYGON((-122.4165 37.7820,-122.4085 37.7820,-122.4085 37.7842,-122.4165 37.7842,-122.4165 37.7820))')&$limit=1000&$order=sf16_bldgid` — **188 records** (all 58 opening-block footprints plus **130 additional measured footprints** extending west across Mason, Taylor, Jones, Leavenworth, and Hyde along Eddy and Turk Streets), downloaded 2026-10-19 UTC. Full intersecting geometries, IDs and properties retained. `data_as_of`/`data_loaded_at` are source/portal timestamps, not new observation dates.
- Transgender Cultural District boundary layer (`public/data/transgender-district.geojson` & `public/data/cultural-district-source.json`): San Francisco Planning Department, **Cultural Districts (`5xmc-5bjj`)**, **PDDL 1.0**, https://data.sf.gov/d/5xmc-5bjj . One complete unmodified MultiPolygon feature (`district_name = "Transgender Cultural District"`, founding year 2017; dataset date `2017-01-01` is a year placeholder, not an exact founding day). Kept as an explicit, toggleable cultural/historical boundary layer within the wider Tenderloin neighborhood.
- Height: `hgt_mediancm / 100` metres above ground, checked against `hgt_median_m`; absolute roof elevation `p2010_zmaxn88ft` is not used. Ground `gnd_mediancm / 100` retained separately in NAVD88 metres; presentation ground currently flat.
- Projection: approximate local metres, origin lat37.7832203/lon-122.410784. X=(lon-origin.lon)*111320*cos(origin.lat); Z=-(lat-origin.lat)*111320. No individual footprint rotation/scaling/simplification. Derived rings omit only their repeated closing vertex. Source precision is not a survey-accuracy guarantee.
- Offline generator: `scripts/build-godot-data.mjs`. Unmodified **Mapbox earcut v3.0.2** triangulates roofs including holes; ISC license at `scripts/vendor/earcut.LICENSE`, https://github.com/mapbox/earcut/tree/v3.0.2 . Build-only, no remote runtime dependency. Each roof's area deviation is checked; all ring walls retained.
- Boeddeker Park outline (`source/boeddeker_park.osm.json`): OpenStreetMap way `33619301` (`leisure=park`, `name=Boeddeker Park`, `addr:housenumber=240`, `addr:street=Eddy Street`), fetched 2026-10-19 UTC from the Overpass API (`way["leisure"="park"](37.7830,-122.4150,37.7860,-122.4100); out geom;`). The closed outer ring is projected with the same origin/formula as the buildings and embedded as `BOEDDEKER_PARK_RING` in `godot/main.gd`. **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, ODbL 1.0. Park furnishings, park trees, rooftop details, corner turrets, and Muni trolley poles/wires are procedural stylizations, not surveyed objects.
- Street trees (`source/street_trees_tkzw-k3nq.json` -> `street_trees.json`): San Francisco Public Works, **Street Tree Inventory (`tkzw-k3nq`)**, **PDDL 1.0**, https://data.sf.gov/d/tkzw-k3nq . Raw SODA response retained unmodified (**498 records**, fetched 2026-10-19): `https://data.sf.gov/resource/tkzw-k3nq.json?$where=latitude between '37.7812' and '37.7850' and longitude between '-122.4172' and '-122.4078'&$order=treeid&$limit=5000` (URL-encode query). `scripts/build-street-trees.mjs` (`npm run godot:trees`) projects each record with the building origin/formula above and keeps **345 trees**. It drops 9 empty "Potential Site" basins (not trees); 50 records stacked 3 or more to one exact coordinate, which are address placeholders rather than surveyed positions (16 of them sit on one point inside Serif, which explains the earlier "18 trees inside `201006.0004056`"); the older record of 9 pairs that share one coordinate; 69 records outside the modeled building area (bounding box + 15 m); and 16 that land inside a current building outline. Four curb-side London planes on Market Street were recorded 0.6–0.9 m inside Livat Mall's OSM outline. The 2023 LiDAR shows that roof starting about 1 m inside the outline and their canopies 1–6 m outside it, so they move straight out to 3.5 m past the wall; each records `moved_m` (about 4.1–4.4 m). The current outline set is the 2010 footprints that still stand plus the post-2010 additions below. Common/scientific species and `mapdbh` trunk diameter are kept; the game sizes and tints canopies from them, but canopy shape and height are stylized. The schematic trolley poles step aside from real trees. `street_trees.json` records the raw file's SHA-256, and `tests/godot-data.test.mjs` re-derives it offline.
- **2023 LiDAR roofs and buildings the 2010 layer lacks** (`lidar_2023.json`, built by `scripts/build-lidar-2023.mjs`, `npm run godot:lidar`). Point cloud: USGS 3D Elevation Program **`CA_SanFrancisco_1_B23`**, collected **2023-04-20** (the decoded GPS timestamps over this block run 11:08–15:11 GPS time that day) and published 2024-08-22. OpenTopography lists 61.80 pts/m² nominal; our crop has about 92 pts/m² with flight-line overlap. License: **US Government Public Domain**, per the AWS Open Data registry entry "USGS 3DEP LiDAR Point Clouds" (bucket `usgs-lidar-public`, us-west-2). It's served as Entwine Point Tiles (LAZ, EPSG:3857): https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/CA_SanFrancisco_1_B23/ept.json . The 287 octree nodes covering the opening block (65.7 MB, not committed) are listed with SHA-256 in `source/lidar_ept_opening_manifest.json`. Classes present: 1 unclassified, 2 ground, 7/18 noise, 17; there's no class 6, so Roofer uses class 1 points inside each footprint and class 2 for ground.
  - Pipeline: `py -3 -I scripts/lidar/fetch_ept.py <dir> -122.41234977630467 -122.40797408369534 37.781842709765 37.784726623234995 godot/data/source/lidar_ept_opening_manifest.json` → `python -I scripts/lidar/crop_points.py <dir> godot/data/buildings.json points.laz 25` (needs `laspy[lazrs]` and numpy; exact inverse Web Mercator, then the game projection) → `node scripts/build-lidar-2023.mjs footprints footprints.geojson` → `roofer -j 16 --bld-class 1 --grnd-class 2 --id-attribute bid --h-terrain-attribute h_terrain --h-terrain-strategy buffer_user --no-lod22 --lod13 --cj-translate 0 0 0 --srs EPSG:32610 points.laz footprints.geojson out` → copy `out/*.city.jsonl` to `source/roofer_opening_lod13.city.jsonl` → `node scripts/build-lidar-2023.mjs`. **Roofer 1.0.0** (`bb2a85a`, 3DBAG / TU Delft 3D geoinformation, GPL-3.0, official Windows release, SHA-256 `143413f1…ae7f` matching GitHub's published digest) runs offline and is not bundled. `EPSG:32610` is only a metric label: the points and footprints are already in the game frame, and Roofer doesn't reproject when the CRS is overridden.
  - Agreement with the 2010 survey: across the 58 opening-block footprints, Roofer's LiDAR ground is within ±1.7 m of the city's NAVD88 ground (median −0.4 m), and the median roof height matches `height_m` within about 1 m for most buildings. The game keeps the 2010 median as each building's base. It adds the LoD 1.3 roof parts that rise at least 1 m above that median (**61 parts**: penthouses, upper wings, towers) to `RooftopDetails`, with a window row per 3.2 m storey. On these measured roofs the rule-placed tanks, penthouses, skylights, vents, and stylized corner turrets are no longer drawn (creator field-survey tags still force theirs). Parts under 4 m² are dropped (81), and so are small parts more than 12 m above the building's 70th-percentile roof (2: neighbor walls, a crane). Footprint `201006.0017255` measures 4.7 m in 2023 against 10.3 m in 2010; it's kept, and its info line says it is lower now.
  - Buildings missing from the 2010 layer: 2023 LiDAR building cells (non-ground points ≥ 3 m above ground, mostly single returns) cover about 24,000 m² of the opening query window, and 16% of that was in no 2010 footprint. Every gap falls inside a current OpenStreetMap building (`source/osm_buildings_opening.json`: raw Overpass response, OSM base 2026-10-20T04:27:56Z, query `[out:json][timeout:90];(way["building"](37.7816,-122.4128,37.7850,-122.4075);relation["building"](37.7816,-122.4128,37.7850,-122.4075););out geom;`; **© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0**). **Livat Mall, 945 Market Street** (way `332515949`) has no 2010 footprint at all and measures 28.2 m. **Serif, 960 Market Street** (way `1072899109`; OSM height 36.576 m, 12 levels) measures 37.7 m. It covers 2010 footprints `201006.0004056`, `201006.0005472`, and `201006.0010048` (71–95% inside), which the 2010 survey has at about 10 m and the 2023 LiDAR at about 37 m. Those three are marked superseded: the game hides them and leaves them out of physics and collision, but keeps their records and ids. The two OSM outlines are drawn at their LiDAR median height with the shared facade builder; their facade colors are stylized. Collision and walking use the current outline set. The OSM-derived outlines are ODbL, like the streets.
- Street lamps are hand-placed and stylized. No city pole inventory was found: DataSF `6tt8-ugnj` ("streetlights") is a log of 311 streetlight service requests, so it only shows poles someone reported, not every pole.
- Muni overhead wires on Turk and Eddy: placed only on streets with real trolleybus service. The 31 Balboa trolleybus runs through the Tenderloin on Eddy and Turk (inbound on Eddy, outbound on Turk between Larkin and Mason), per the SFMTA route page (https://SFMTA.com/31, checked 2026-10-19) and Wikipedia "31 Balboa" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/31_Balboa). No Muni rail or streetcar runs on these streets, and none is modeled. Pole spacing and lane side are schematic.
- Streets: existing `public/data/streets.osm.json` plus source XML (`source-turk-west.osm.xml` and `source.osm.xml`), original OSM IDs/coordinates across all 7 named streets (`Turk`, `Taylor`, `Mason`, `Jones`, `Leavenworth`, `Hyde`, `Eddy`) and shared Turk intersection nodes (`Jones 65365830`, `Leavenworth 65362154`, `Hyde 65360270`). **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright , ODbL 1.0. Derived street data retains source metadata and ODbL; city buildings and cultural district remain PDDL. The 10 m bands are schematic widths, not surveyed curbs/sidewalks/permissions.

`source_receipt.json` records input/vendor hashes and official engine/template asset hashes; tests verify every listed hash. Generated `buildings.json` records raw GeoJSON, wider GeoJSON, cultural district, and OSM street hashes, and tests verify exact derived streets, footprints, and district rings. Godot Engine copyright/permission text is retained at `godot/ENGINE_LICENSE.txt` and included in the export. No imagery, business identities, current occupancy claims, business ratings or reviews, borrowed art/music or Google Maps/Earth assets imported. Current façades/roof details/entrances/crossings/decorations remain unverified. The six eateries are fictional and labeled "(fictional)" in the scene. On 2026-10-19 their names were checked against real San Francisco businesses, and three were renamed to avoid confusion: "Golden Lotus" (a real restaurant at 631 Larkin St) became Bowl & Basil Pho Counter, "Midnight Sun" (a real Castro bar) became Open Seat Diner & Pie Counter, and the owl-themed "Brass Owl" tavern (close to The Owl Tree at 601 Post St) became Copper Cue Tavern & Billiards. The made-up star ratings were removed.

## Why Google Maps / Google Open Buildings Are Not Bundled (Even Non-Commercial)

- **Google Open Buildings V3 (`CC BY 4.0`)**: Covers Africa, South/Southeast Asia, Latin America, and the Caribbean; it does **not** cover North America or San Francisco.
- **Google Maps Platform Photorealistic 3D Tiles, Street View, and Places**: Even for non-commercial projects, Google Maps Platform requires a live billed Google Cloud API key at runtime, and Google Maps Platform Terms of Service (Section 3.2.3: *No Scraping*, *No Caching*, *No Creating Content From Google Maps Content*) prohibit pre-fetching, extracting, or redistributing Google 3D meshes, textures, panoramas, or place data inside a self-contained offline MIT repository.
- **Verified Open-Data Alternative Used Here**: San Francisco's official airborne-LiDAR + Pictometry building footprints (`DataSF ynuv-fyni`, **PDDL 1.0**) paired with **OpenStreetMap (`ODbL 1.0`)** street centerlines and the **Cultural Districts (`DataSF 5xmc-5bjj`, PDDL 1.0)** boundary give higher cadastral/roof-height fidelity across the Tenderloin without proprietary keys, runtime network calls, or ToS restrictions.
