# Building geography provenance

City and County of San Francisco, Department of Technology, **Building Footprints**, **ynuv-fyni**. Raw GeoJSON, original metadata and attached method PDF are retained in `source/`.

- Catalog: https://data.sfgov.org/Geographic-Locations-and-Boundaries/Building-Footprints/ynuv-fyni
- Metadata: https://data.sfgov.org/api/views/ynuv-fyni.json
- PDF: https://data.sfgov.org/api/views/ynuv-fyni/files/e6e73cd0-15be-47a8-9463-9c9a725b72c3?download=true&filename=SF_BldgFoot_2017-05_description.pdf
- Method prepared 2017-05-01; Pictometry **2010** footprints and airborne LiDAR. Later parcel-guided splitting is not a new survey. 50 cm feature-height surface subtracts bare earth from first return; may include roof/tree effects. Median is a summary, not a detailed roof.
- Published license: **Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL) 1.0**, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1.0/ . Confirmed in metadata and PDF. Repository MIT does not override data licenses.
- Opening-block query (`source/buildings.geojson`): `https://data.sfgov.org/resource/ynuv-fyni.geojson?$where=intersects(shape,'POLYGON((-122.4118 37.7825,-122.4085 37.7825,-122.4085 37.7842,-122.4118 37.7842,-122.4118 37.7825))')&$limit=1000&$order=sf16_bldgid` (URL-encode query) — **58 records** around Turk/Taylor/Mason.
- Wider Tenderloin corridor query (`source/buildings_tenderloin_west.geojson`): `https://data.sfgov.org/resource/ynuv-fyni.geojson?$where=intersects(shape,'POLYGON((-122.4165 37.7820,-122.4085 37.7820,-122.4085 37.7842,-122.4165 37.7842,-122.4165 37.7820))')&$limit=1000&$order=sf16_bldgid` — **188 records** (all 58 opening-block footprints plus **130 additional measured footprints** extending west across Mason, Taylor, Jones, Leavenworth, and Hyde along Eddy and Turk Streets), downloaded 2026-10-19 UTC. Full intersecting geometries, IDs and properties retained. `data_as_of`/`data_loaded_at` are source/portal timestamps, not new observation dates.
- Transgender Cultural District boundary layer (`public/data/transgender-district.geojson` & `public/data/cultural-district-source.json`): San Francisco Planning Department, **Cultural Districts (`5xmc-5bjj`)**, **PDDL 1.0**, https://data.sf.gov/d/5xmc-5bjj . One complete unmodified MultiPolygon feature (`district_name = "Transgender Cultural District"`, founding year 2017; dataset date `2017-01-01` is a year placeholder, not an exact founding day). Kept as an explicit, toggleable cultural/historical boundary layer within the wider Tenderloin neighborhood.
- Height: `hgt_mediancm / 100` metres above ground, checked against `hgt_median_m`; absolute roof elevation `p2010_zmaxn88ft` is not used. Ground `gnd_mediancm / 100` retained separately in NAVD88 metres; presentation ground currently flat.
- Projection: approximate local metres, origin lat37.7832203/lon-122.410784. X=(lon-origin.lon)*111320*cos(origin.lat); Z=-(lat-origin.lat)*111320. No individual footprint rotation/scaling/simplification. Derived rings omit only their repeated closing vertex. Source precision is not a survey-accuracy guarantee.
- Offline generator: `scripts/build-godot-data.mjs`. Unmodified **Mapbox earcut v3.0.2** triangulates roofs including holes; ISC license at `scripts/vendor/earcut.LICENSE`, https://github.com/mapbox/earcut/tree/v3.0.2 . Build-only, no remote runtime dependency. Each roof's area deviation is checked; all ring walls retained.
- Boeddeker Park outline (`source/boeddeker_park.osm.json`): OpenStreetMap way `33619301` (`leisure=park`, `name=Boeddeker Park`, `addr:housenumber=240`, `addr:street=Eddy Street`), fetched 2026-10-19 UTC from the Overpass API (`way["leisure"="park"](37.7830,-122.4150,37.7860,-122.4100); out geom;`). The closed outer ring is projected with the same origin/formula as the buildings and embedded as `BOEDDEKER_PARK_RING` in `godot/main.gd`. **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, ODbL 1.0. Park furnishings, park trees, rooftop details, corner turrets, and Muni trolley poles/wires are procedural stylizations, not surveyed objects.
- Street trees (`source/street_trees_tkzw-k3nq.json` -> `street_trees.json`): San Francisco Public Works, **Street Tree Inventory (`tkzw-k3nq`)**, **PDDL 1.0**, https://data.sf.gov/d/tkzw-k3nq . Raw SODA response retained unmodified (**498 records**, fetched 2026-10-19): `https://data.sf.gov/resource/tkzw-k3nq.json?$where=latitude between '37.7812' and '37.7850' and longitude between '-122.4172' and '-122.4078'&$order=treeid&$limit=5000` (URL-encode query). `scripts/build-street-trees.mjs` (`npm run godot:trees`) projects each record with the building origin/formula above and keeps **345 trees**. It drops 9 empty "Potential Site" basins (not trees); 50 records stacked 3 or more to one exact coordinate, which are address placeholders rather than surveyed positions (16 of them sit on one point inside Serif, which explains the earlier "18 trees inside `201006.0004056`"); the older record of 9 pairs that share one coordinate; 69 records outside the modeled building area (bounding box + 15 m); and 16 that land inside a current building outline. Four curb-side London planes on Market Street were recorded 0.6–0.9 m inside Livat Mall's OSM outline. The 2023 LiDAR shows that roof starting about 1 m inside the outline and their canopies 1–6 m outside it, so they move straight out to 3.5 m past the wall; each records `moved_m` (about 4.1–4.4 m). The current outline set is the 2010 footprints that still stand plus the additions below. Common/scientific species and `mapdbh` trunk diameter are kept; the game sizes and tints canopies from them, but canopy shape and height are stylized. The schematic trolley poles step aside from real trees. `street_trees.json` records the raw file's SHA-256, and `tests/godot-data.test.mjs` re-derives it offline.
- **2023 LiDAR roofs and current buildings** (`lidar_2023.json`, built by `scripts/build-lidar-2023.mjs`, `npm run godot:lidar`), covering all 188 footprints. Point cloud: USGS 3D Elevation Program **`CA_SanFrancisco_1_B23`**, collected **2023-04-20** (the decoded GPS timestamps over these blocks run 11:08–15:11 GPS time that day) and published 2024-08-22. OpenTopography lists 61.80 pts/m² nominal; our crops average about 90 pts/m² with flight-line overlap. License: **US Government Public Domain**, per the AWS Open Data registry entry "USGS 3DEP LiDAR Point Clouds" (bucket `usgs-lidar-public`, us-west-2). It's served as Entwine Point Tiles (LAZ, EPSG:3857): https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/CA_SanFrancisco_1_B23/ept.json . The 650 octree nodes covering the modeled area (149.0 MB, 31.1 M points, not committed) are listed with SHA-256 in `source/lidar_ept_manifest.json`. Classes present: 1 unclassified, 2 ground, 7/18 noise, 17; there's no class 6, so Roofer uses class 1 points inside each footprint and class 2 for ground.
  - Pipeline: `py -3 -I scripts/lidar/fetch_ept.py <dir> -122.4171286 -122.4079173 37.7813557 37.7847715 godot/data/source/lidar_ept_manifest.json` (all footprints + 25 m) → `python -I scripts/lidar/crop_points.py <dir> godot/data/buildings.json points.laz 25` (needs `laspy[lazrs]` and numpy; exact inverse Web Mercator, then the game projection) → `node scripts/build-lidar-2023.mjs footprints footprints.geojson` → Roofer pass 1: `roofer -j 16 --bld-class 1 --grnd-class 2 --id-attribute bid --h-terrain-attribute h_terrain --h-terrain-strategy buffer_user --no-lod22 --lod13 --cj-translate 0 0 0 --srs EPSG:32610 points.laz footprints.geojson out` → copy `out/*.city.jsonl` to `source/roofer_lod13.city.jsonl` → `node scripts/build-lidar-2023.mjs pass2 footprints_pass2.geojson` (prints the `--filter`) → Roofer pass 2 with that `--filter` on `footprints_pass2.geojson` (the exact command is recorded as `source.roofer.pass2_command` in `lidar_2023.json`) → copy to `source/roofer_lod13_pass2.city.jsonl` → `node scripts/build-lidar-2023.mjs`. **Roofer 1.0.0** (`bb2a85a`, 3DBAG / TU Delft 3D geoinformation, GPL-3.0, official Windows release, SHA-256 `143413f1…ae7f` matching GitHub's published digest) runs offline and is not bundled. `EPSG:32610` is only a metric label: the points and footprints are already in the game frame, and Roofer doesn't reproject when the CRS is overridden. Pass 2 exists for two reasons. In the full-area run Roofer flagged 3 footprints' points as insufficient, although the same command limited to them reconstructs them normally (within 0.4 m of their 2010 heights). And the OSM outlines of rebuilt sites only become known after pass 1.
  - Heights: the game measures every LiDAR height from the city's surveyed median ground (`ground_m_navd88`). Additions use the median ground of the 2010 footprints they replace or that lie within 40 m. Roofer's own ground (5th percentile within 4 m of the footprint) agrees on flat blocks (median offset −0.6 m), but on sloped blocks it sits up to 5.5 m low, which would inflate heights. With the city ground, 80% of standing 2010 footprints match the 2023 LiDAR median within 0.56 m and 95% within 2.3 m.
  - Roofs: the game keeps the 2010 median as each building's base. It adds the LoD 1.3 roof parts that rise at least 1 m above that median (**125 parts**: penthouses, upper wings, towers) to `RooftopDetails`, with a window row per 3.2 m storey. No rule-placed tanks, penthouses, skylights, vents, or stylized corner turrets remain on any roof (creator field-survey tags still force theirs). Parts under 4 m² are dropped (258), and so are small parts more than 12 m above the building's 70th-percentile roof (9: neighbor walls, cranes, trees). Two footprints under 20 m² are marked too small for a height check. Nine standing or replaced footprints differ from 2010 by more than 5 m. Those not replaced keep the 2010 base, with an info line saying "taller/lower than in 2010" (for example `201006.0017255`: 4.0 m in 2023 against 10.3 m in 2010).
  - Current buildings (`source/osm_buildings.json`: raw Overpass response, OSM base 2026-10-20T05:05:48Z, query `[out:json][timeout:90];(way["building"](37.7813,-122.4172,37.7848,-122.4078);relation["building"](37.7813,-122.4172,37.7848,-122.4078););out geom;`; **© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0**). 2023 LiDAR building cells (non-ground points ≥ 3 m above ground, mostly single returns) cover about 78,000 m² of the city's wider query window; 9.8% of that lay in no 2010 footprint. The rules:
    - **Missing**: an OSM outline whose centroid is in the window and that the 2010 footprints cover less than 40% is added. That gives **Livat Mall, 945 Market St** (way `332515949`, 27.8 m), **TL Residences, 361 Turk St** (way `1059931715`, 8 levels, 25.7 m), **TL Residences, 145 Leavenworth St** (way `1258857968`, 8 levels, 25.1 m), **180 Jones St** (way `1258513962`, 9 levels, 25.5 m), and an unnamed building (way `1056296141`, 12.6 m).
    - **Rebuilt**: an OSM outline that contains at least 70% of a 2010 footprint whose LiDAR median differs from 2010 by more than 5 m replaces it. That gives **Serif, 960 Market St** (way `1072899109`, OSM height 36.576 m, 12 levels; LiDAR 36.7 m), which supersedes `201006.0004056`, `0005472`, and `0010048` (~10 m in 2010); **Prism, 1028 Market St** (way `32864957`, 13 levels, 38.6 m), which supersedes `201006.0002011` (9.7 m in 2010); and **993 Market St** (way `332516647`, 1 level, 5.6 m), which supersedes `201006.0021077` (11.8 m in 2010).
    - The 3,881 m² of uncovered LiDAR building area inside these additions is now modeled. Unresolved: about 866 m² lies in no OSM building, mostly an ~805 m², ~5 m-high structure near (-366, 6) north of Turk between Leavenworth and Hyde, so it isn't modeled (no outline source). Another 46 m² is a sliver of 50 Jones St.
    - Superseded footprints are hidden and leave physics and collision but keep their records and ids. Additions are drawn at their LiDAR median with the shared facade builder; their facade colors are stylized, except the TL Residences, whose terracotta/buff palette (formerly applied by a coordinate box to five unrelated 2010 footprints) now goes to the real buildings. The creator's "So What" easter egg moved onto the real facades: the 8th-floor window on 145 Leavenworth's 8th floor (the LiDAR height split over its 8 OSM storeys), and the awning on 361 Turk's Turk frontage. Collision and walking use the current outline set. The OSM-derived outlines are ODbL, like the streets.

Derived street data retains source metadata and ODbL; city buildings and cultural district remain PDDL. The 10 m bands are schematic widths, not surveyed curbs/sidewalks/permissions.

`source_receipt.json` records input/vendor hashes and official engine/template asset hashes; tests verify every listed hash. ## Why Google Maps / Google Open Buildings Are Not Bundled (Even Non-Commercial)

- **Google Open Buildings V3 (`CC BY 4.0`)**: Covers Africa, South/Southeast Asia, Latin America, and the Caribbean; it does **not** cover North America or San Francisco.
- **Google Maps Platform Photorealistic 3D Tiles, Street View, and Places**: Even for non-commercial projects, Google Maps Platform requires a live billed Google Cloud API key at runtime, and Google Maps Platform Terms of Service (Section 3.2.3: *No Scraping*, *No Caching*, *No Creating Content From Google Maps Content*) prohibit pre-fetching, extracting, or redistributing Google 3D meshes, textures, panoramas, or place data inside a self-contained offline MIT repository.
- **Verified Open-Data Alternative Used Here**: San Francisco's official airborne-LiDAR + Pictometry building footprints (`DataSF ynuv-fyni`, **PDDL 1.0**) paired with **OpenStreetMap (`ODbL 1.0`)** street centerlines and the **Cultural Districts (`DataSF 5xmc-5bjj`, PDDL 1.0)** boundary give higher cadastral/roof-height fidelity across the Tenderloin without proprietary keys, runtime network calls, or ToS restrictions.
