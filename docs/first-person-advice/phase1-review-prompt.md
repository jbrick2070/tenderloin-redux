## What the change is

The 3D edition (`godot/main.gd`, about 16,000 lines) has always used an elevated orthographic "map view" camera. The change adds an optional eye-level "street view", Phase 1 of `docs/FIRST_PERSON_PLAN.md` (read sections 4, 5 "Phase 1", 6, 7 and 10 first; section 10 has the measured Phase 0 results).

Files to read in this order:
1. `godot/street_view.gd` (new, about 300 lines): the module. One rule, `active()`, decides whether the eye camera is in charge: the player chose it (`enabled`), the map view is following the player, nothing is framed, selected or focused, and the player and any route goal are on the opening block (x >= -50). `sync()` applies enter/exit side effects once when that answer changes. `apply_camera()` is the street branch of `update_camera()`. The module also owns the floating "Street view / Map view" button, the snap turn (Shift+Left/Right, 30°), tilt (Shift+Up/Down, 15°), a 2D line-of-sight test against building footprints for captions, a sky and depth fog applied on entry, and the a11y snapshot.
2. The hooks in `godot/main.gd`. Find them with `git diff HEAD -- godot/main.gd` if the change is uncommitted, or `git show --stat HEAD` and `git log -3 --oneline` if it is committed; search for `street_view` otherwise. They are: `var street_view`; creation in `make_ui`; the street branch at the top of `update_camera()`; `street_view.tick()` in `_process`; `panel_camera_pose()` gaining a fifth element; early returns in `refit_panel_camera`, `refresh_hub_camera`, `update_occlusion`, `zoom`, `pan_camera_by_screen_delta`, `apply_map_pinch`; `rotate_camera_by_delta` delegating to `street_view.look`; `screen_constant_scale(px, at)` gaining a position argument and a perspective branch (every caller now passes a position); `speech_bubble_base_scale` using the frustum height at the bubble's depth; `update_readable_scene_labels` hiding captions beyond 28 m or without line of sight; `is_screen_point_in_ui` and the label `occupied` list including the button; `_physics_process` rotating keyboard walking by the street yaw, skipping the map's smooth Shift+arrow orbit and the web ×2.2 route multiplier while active; `screen_to_ground_xz` returning `Vector2(INF, INF)` at or above the horizon or beyond 60 m in perspective, with `handle_scene_tap` returning early on that; the `KEY_LEFT/RIGHT/UP/DOWN` and `KEY_F` arms in `_unhandled_input` (Shift+F toggles; plain F is still Find Me); `player_screen_point()` for the a11y snapshot; `"street_view"` in the snapshot.
3. `godot/tests/street_view_checks.gd` (new; preloaded from `godot/tests/study_test.gd` right after `before_streets_checks`), and `tests/street-view-browser.mjs` (new; called from `tests/godot-browser.mjs` after "Start Exploring" at 1100×760, 390×667 and 320×667).
