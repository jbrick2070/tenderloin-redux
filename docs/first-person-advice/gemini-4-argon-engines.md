## 2. Grounding in This Repository: Why Porting Costs What It Does

Inspecting [`godot/project.godot`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/project.godot#L1-L19) and [`godot/main.gd`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L1-L16327) reveals why the existing `.pck` is only **~1.47 MB** and why porting away from Godot would mean rewriting almost the entire application rather than just converting 3D model files:

1. **Zero Baked 3D Models — 100% Procedural GDScript City Generation:**
   - The 188 measured buildings (`58` opening block + `130` wider Tenderloin from `res://data/buildings.json`), 2023 USGS LiDAR roof masses ([`load_lidar_data()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L714-L746), [`add_lidar_roof_masses()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L748-L837)), and SF Street Tree Inventory ([`load_street_tree_data()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L5257-L5293), [`build_street_trees()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L5295-L5379)) are stored as compact JSON coordinate arrays.
   - At startup ([`_ready()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L1929-L2080)), [`make_building_mesh_and_rect()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L1682-L1916) and [`build_rooftop_details()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L839-L1078) extrude every storefront, sloped fabric awning, projecting SF bay window, wrought-iron fire escape, cornice, water tank, and skylight in code using Godot's `SurfaceTool` and a custom spatial shader ([`BUILDING_SHADER_CODE`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L1080-L1091)).
   - Landmarks and props—including [`build_streets()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L2082-L2209), [`build_mural_board()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L2643-L2771), [`build_rook_speaker()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L2773-L2846), [`build_boeddeker_park()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L5200-L5255), [`build_muni_overhead_lines()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L5396-L5476), [`build_eatery_counters()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L5525-L5618), and [`build_neighborhood_surprises()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L5620-L5793)—are likewise built out of procedural `SurfaceTool` and primitive meshes.

Switching engines requires either rewriting ~4,500 lines of procedural `SurfaceTool` mesh-generation code in the target language or baking the procedural city to `.glb` (which would bloat the download well past your 2 MiB asset budget).

2. **Real-World Metre Scale & Street-Level Collision Already Exist:**

## 3. Recommendation: Stay on Godot 4.7.2 (`gl_compatibility`) and Add a First-Person Mode

**Verdict:** **Stay on Godot 4.** For a one-person project with a ~1.47 MB `.pck` budget, two editions already sharing `localStorage`, and 17,000+ lines of working procedural street architecture and neighborhood stories, adding a street-level first-person camera mode inside [`godot/main.gd`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd) is a **1–2 week surgical feature (~300–600 lines of GDScript)** with **zero bytes of extra asset download**, whereas switching engines is a **3–6+ month rewrite**.

### Why Godot 4 Wins Against Every Hard Constraint

1. **Zero Asset Bloating (< 2 MiB `.pck` Constraint):**
   - Because [`make_building_mesh_and_rect()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L1682-L1916) already generates ground-floor storefronts, awnings, bay windows, fire escapes, curbs, crosswalks, and trees in 3D at 1:1 metre scale, switching [`camera`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L2058-L2062) to perspective projection at eye height (`1.58 m`) requires **no new textures or meshes**. Adding 300–600 lines of GDScript adds **< 15 KB** to the `.pck`, keeping it comfortably around ~1.48 MB.
2. **Exact ` main.gd` Touchpoints to Add First-Person Mode (Low Risk, Reversible):**
   - **Camera Projection & Eye Pose ([`update_camera()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L15098-L15129)):** Currently, [`camera`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L2058-L2062) is always `Camera3D.PROJECTION_ORTHOGONAL` positioned at `focus + CAMERA_OFFSET`. In first-person mode (`first_person_mode == true` and `not in_overview and not in_wide_overview`), set `camera.projection = Camera3D.PROJECTION_PERSPECTIVE`, `camera.fov = 70.0`, `camera.near = 0.15`, `camera.far = 650.0`, position it at `player.position + Vector3(0.0, PLAYER_HEIGHT_M * 0.92, 0.0)`, and orient it by `fp_yaw` and clamped `fp_pitch` (`±65°`).
   - **Player Mesh & Building Cutaway ([`update_building_cutaway()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L14112-L14174)):** Hide [`player_visual`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L2626-L2633) when in first-person (`player_visual.visible = not first_person_mode`) and call [`clear_occlusion()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L14065-L14085) so nearby buildings are not made translucent by the orthographic ray-slope check in [`building_record_occludes_point()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L14087-L14110).
   - **3D Labels & Speech Bubble Scaling ([`speech_bubble_base_scale()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L4690-L4692), [`screen_constant_scale()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L4694-L4700), [`update_readable_labels_for_view()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L15131-L15165)):** Those helpers currently scale by `camera.size` (orthographic height in metres). In perspective mode, replace `camera.size` with `2.0 * cam_dist * tan(deg_to_rad(camera.fov * 0.5))` (or enable `Label3D.fixed_size` / distance clamping) so neighbor nameplates, street signs, and speech bubbles stay crisp at street level.
   - **Browser Pointer Lock, Touch Look, & Keyboard ([`_unhandled_input()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L14987-L15096), [`_physics_process()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L8383-L8600)):**
     - **Desktop:** Request `Input.set_mouse_mode(Input.MOUSE_MODE_CAPTURED)` on canvas click in first-person view (and automatically release to `MOUSE_MODE_VISIBLE` whenever a conversation panel, story card, or modal opens, or when pressing `Esc`).
     - **Phone / Tablet:** [`_unhandled_input()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L14987-L15096) already tracks `InputEventScreenTouch` and `InputEventScreenDrag` for pinch/pan. In first-person mode, single-finger drag rotates `fp_yaw` / `fp_pitch`, while tap-to-walk ([`start_click_walk()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L8367-L8381)) and the bottom [`walk_control`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L16076) button continue to work without requiring twin-stick dexterity.
     - **Keyboard:** `W`/`S` or `Up`/`Down` step forward/back along `Vector2(-sin(fp_yaw), -cos(fp_yaw))` via [`walk_step()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L8156-L8266); `A`/`D` strafe; `Q`/`E` or `Left`/`Right` turn yaw smoothly (or snap-turn by `30°`/`45°` when reduced motion is enabled).
3. **Accessibility & Reduced Motion:**
   - First-person view can coexist as a **toggleable camera mode** (`Street View (V)` ↔ `Overhead Walk` ↔ `Tenderloin Map`), so players who experience motion sickness in perspective 3D can switch back to the existing orthographic walk or use **click-to-walk / auto-walk (`Walk to Next`)** and **snap-turning** with zero head-bob.
   - All existing screen-reader live announcements ([`announce_a11y_status()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L12028-L12035)) and keyboard-navigable `Control` panels remain untouched.
4. **Later WebXR Path:**
   - Godot 4's `gl_compatibility` renderer is the exact renderer Godot uses for **WebXR** (`WebXRInterface`) and standalone Quest builds. Adding an `XROrigin3D` + `XRCamera3D` node tree later lets the player stand on Turk Street in a Quest browser using the exact same `SurfaceTool` buildings, `MultiMeshInstance3D` street trees, and `AudioStreamGenerator` 80 BPM music. *(Note: existing 2D `CanvasLayer` UI panels would need to be rendered onto a `SubViewport` quad in 3D space when inside a WebXR session.)*

## 4. Conditions Under Which You Should Switch Engines

While staying on Godot 4.7.2 is the clear winner today, you should consider switching to **Babylon.js** (or **three.js** if you prefer a minimal library) **only if one or more of the following conditions become true**:

1. **You Decide to Merge the 2D (`browser-next/`) and 3D (`godot/`) Codebases into a Single Codebase:**
   - Right now, you maintain neighborhood stories, save schemas, and activities in both plain JavaScript (`browser-next/`) and GDScript (`godot/`). If maintaining two implementations becomes unsustainable for a solo developer and you want **one JavaScript codebase** where the 2D map, 3D overhead view, 3D street-level view, and native HTML/DOM UI are all driven by the exact same JS modules and native HTML DOM accessibility tree, porting the 3D renderer into `browser-next/` using **Babylon.js** or **three.js** eliminates the dual-codebase tax.
2. **Total Initial Download Size (Including `.wasm`) Must Be Under 3 MB Over Cellular:**
   - Even though your `.pck` is ~1.47 MB, first-time visitors to the Godot web build also download the Godot WebAssembly binary (`godot.web.template_release.wasm32.wasm`), which is typically **~5–8 MB compressed** (~3–4 MB if you compile a custom stripped export template). If you require the *entire* cold-cache initial payload (engine + assets) to be **< 2–3 MB** on low-end phones, a native JS engine (**three.js** at ~150 KB gzipped or **Babylon.js** tree-shaken) is the only way to eliminate the WebAssembly engine binary.
3. **WebXR Becomes the Primary Mode and Requires Deep Browser-Native XR Features:**
   - Godot 4's `WebXRInterface` works well for stereoscopic 3D viewing and controller raycasts, but **Babylon.js (`WebXRExperienceHelper`)** has deeper out-of-the-box browser WebXR support—including WebXR DOM Overlay, hand-mesh tracking, WebXR Layers for crisp text, and built-in comfort vignettes/teleportation rings—and avoids rendering Godot's `CanvasLayer` UI into `SubViewport` 3D quads.
4. **Native HTML Screen-Reader Navigation Inside 3D/XR Becomes Mandatory:**
   - Godot's canvas is a single `<canvas>` element; [`setup_web_a11y_bridge()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/godot/main.gd#L11456-L11508) bridges screen readers well via hidden live ARIA regions, but native semantic HTML buttons/dialogs (`<button>`, `<dialog>`, CSS `prefers-reduced-motion`) over a **Babylon.js** or **three.js** `<canvas>` give VoiceOver (iOS), TalkBack (Android), and NVDA/JAWS direct spatial touch-exploration of every button without a custom bridge.
