# First-person execution spec: Phases 2–7

**Status:** second draft, November 2, 2026, after two outside reviews of the first draft (`docs/first-person-advice/execution-review-codex.md`, `execution-review-gemini.md`) and their source-checked synthesis (`execution-review-final.md`): 17 anchor corrections and five replacement texts were taken, with every anchor re-verified against the tree before it was written here. Written on the Phase 1 tree (commits 8bba1b6 and 103cb2f plus the review follow-ups in the working tree). Line numbers are for that tree and will drift; function names are the real anchors. This document turns the roadmap in `docs/FIRST_PERSON_PLAN.md` into work orders a fresh session can execute one phase at a time, with the tests, exits and open decisions for each. Where it disagrees with the plan, this document wins; where it is silent, the plan applies.

## 0. Ground rules for every phase

- **One rule owns the camera.** `street_view.active()` (`godot/street_view.gd`) is the only predicate that decides perspective versus the elevated map. Phases add terms to that rule or new resume paths; they never set `camera.projection` anywhere else. Every enter/exit side effect goes through `enter()` / `exit()` so `sync()` stays idempotent.
- **Off means byte-for-byte.** With `enabled == false` the map view must behave exactly as before the phase. Every hook in `main.gd` is guarded by `street_view_active()` or `street_view != null`. The one deliberate exception is the reduced-motion setting (Phase 2), which also stills map-view animations when the player turns it on; it is off by default, so the default path is still unchanged. The formal suite (`npm run godot:test`), the browser suite (`npm run godot:browser`) and the three 2D suites must pass unchanged in a clean checkout before a push (see section 9).
- **Transient state only.** Nothing about the eye (mode, heading, pitch, capture) is saved. Settings added in Phase 2 are the only persistence and go through the existing v1 envelope (`save_godot_settings` 10713, `load_godot_settings` 10719), optional keys with safe defaults.
- **Phone first.** Every new control has a 44 px touch target and an announced name; every new world element has a text equivalent in the a11y bridge (`announce_a11y_status` 12031, `get_a11y_status_snapshot` 11838) and in `tests/godot-browser.mjs` at 1100×760, 390×667 and 320×667.
- **Budget.** The pck stays under 2 MiB (now 1,489,888 bytes with the Phase 1 code). Measure after each phase; keep 200 KB of headroom. No new textures or audio.
- **Reviews.** Each phase lands with: Opus (or the current model) implementing with fail-first tests, a Sonnet QA pass with the three lenses (stability, map-view regression, test quality), an outside review prompt written to `docs/first-person-advice/<phase>-review-prompt.md` (the creator runs it in Codex and agy), findings taken line by line, then the clean-checkout run.

## 2. Phase 2: look and movement controls

**Goal.** Walking and looking feel right on desktop and on a phone, with mouse capture as a strict opt-in.

**Decisions (defaults in italics; creator may override before the phase starts).** Turning: *snap 30° on keys and buttons, smooth for drag*. Mouselook: *opt-in, off by default, never requested automatically*. Reduced motion: *a real setting, seeded from `prefers-reduced-motion`, saved as an override*. Portrait FOV: *keep the horizontal field constant (`Camera3D.KEEP_WIDTH`) so 320×667 is not a 37° slit*.

**Work orders.**

1. **Contact roles (mouse and touch).** `main.gd` `_unhandled_input` mouse section (`pointer_active` / `pointer_dragging` / `pointer_down_pos` / `pointer_last_pos`, 15135–15165; the pan dispatch is 15164) and `map_pinch_input` 14622, which owns `map_touch_contacts`. While `street_view_active()`, every contact gets a role at contact-down: GUI (a Control under it), movement (inside the stick), look (anywhere else in the play area), or ignored (a third contact, or a second look). One movement and one look may coexist in either order; a second contact never starts the map pinch. A look drag past the 8 px threshold calls `street_view.look(-d.x * 0.0085, d.y * 0.0035)`; a release under 8 px is a tap into `handle_scene_tap`; a release after a drag never routes. Godot emulates mouse events from touch: an owned touch contact suppresses its emulated-mouse duplicate (`event.device == InputEvent.DEVICE_ID_EMULATION`). `cancel_map_pinch` clears the roles and is already called on every mode flip; also clear them on focus loss (`_notification` 10945). Keep `settle_swallows` 14734 untouched: it guards double activations, not drags.
2. **Touch stick.** New `Control` in `street_view.gd` (`StreetStick`, bottom-left, 120×120, visible only when `DisplayServer.is_touchscreen_available()` and active). Its contact owns movement; each physics frame the stick's vector (deadzone 0.15, clamped to 1.0) is rotated by `walk_input()` and fed through the existing path in `_physics_process` 14356 → `walk_step` 8311 with a magnitude. `walk_step` normalizes its input today; add an optional `magnitude: float = 1.0` that scales `total_dist` (default unchanged). Keys win over the stick when both are active; panels and focused activities guard the stick the way they guard keys.
3. **Keyboard.** Already relative to yaw; Shift+Up/Down pitch exists. The snap step becomes a setting (`street_turn`: `"snap30"`, `"snap45"`, `"smooth"`, with a smooth rate of 90°/s on held Shift+arrows).
4. **Opt-in mouse capture.** Measured in Phase 0: Chrome engages in 3–6 ms from a press, Esc reaches Godot as `KEY_ESCAPE`, a re-lock 0.5 s later succeeds, a tab switch releases the lock and `Input.mouse_mode` tracks it; an embedded webview refused it entirely. Design: a "Mouse look" toggle button in the street controls plus a `street_mouselook` setting (no letter key: every letter is bound). The request is made synchronously inside the press handler (`Input.mouse_mode = MOUSE_MODE_CAPTURED` from `_unhandled_input` on a left press while the toggle is on and nothing is under the pointer) and tracked as asynchronous: `capture_state` goes `requested` → `locked` when `Input.mouse_mode` reports captured, or → `denied` if it does not within 10 frames (announce "The browser did not allow mouse look" and turn the toggle off), or → `released` when the browser drops it (tab switch). A grant that arrives after the player has left street view or opened a panel is cancelled immediately. While locked, Esc is consumed by the capture (release, announce "Mouse released") before any gameplay Esc handling. Release before every panel, map, activity and on focus loss (`exit()` runs on every suspension; add the release there and in `_notification`), and never re-request automatically. `InputEventMouseMotion.screen_relative` drives `look()` while locked. Announce "Esc releases the mouse" on lock.
5. **Settings.** Extend the v1 envelope with optional `reduced_motion: bool`, `street_fov: int` (60–90, default 70), `street_turn: String`, `street_mouselook: bool`. `load_godot_settings` 10719 ignores unknown keys today; validate the new keys like the existing two and fall back to defaults on anything invalid. Precedence for reduced motion: a saved override wins; with no saved value the OS preference seeds it; "Reset to system" clears the override. Never persist `street_enabled`, `capture_state`, yaw or pitch.
6. **Reduced motion.** Seed from `JavaScriptBridge.eval("matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches")` once at startup on web. When on: every camera transition is a cut (no lerps are added by this plan anyway), the bubble bob (`update_speech_bubble` 4798, the `sin(... * 2.1)` term), the marker spin and bob (`update_guide_marker` 6279), the tree sway (`update_tree_sway` 5064) and the celebration burst spin (`update_celebration_burst` 5936) are stilled. A "Reduced motion" toggle in the header's Info panel.
7. **Portrait FOV.** `apply_camera()`: the chosen angle (`street_fov`, default 70°) is vertical in landscape (`KEEP_HEIGHT`) and horizontal in portrait (`KEEP_WIDTH`); `enter()` saves the camera's `keep_aspect` and `exit()` restores it (today only `near` and `far` are saved, S:73–74). `screen_constant()` uses the matching dimension (`tan(fov/2)` against height or width). Test fixed-size labels first (pixel size of a `Label3D` at 3 m equal at 320×667 and 1100×760 within 10 %), then the responsive bubble, whose own scale factor already differs between those sizes (`speech_bubble_screen_scale` 4685).

**Tests (`street_view_checks.gd`, new groups 14–20).** Yaw-relative movement at 0°, 90°, 180° moves the player along the expected axes; diagonal input is normalized; a synthetic left-drag changes yaw and leaves `following_player` true and the player in place; a drag release under 8 px routes (taps) and over 8 px does not; a stick contact moves and its release stops; the look contact never starts a pinch (`map_pinch_active` stays false); capture state is released by `frame_conversation`, `wide_overview` and a focused activity opening; settings round-trip with defaults for an old envelope; reduced motion stills the four animations: the tree canopy transforms, the bubble's bob offset and the marker's spin and bob are constant across 30 frames once the bubble's 0.2 s pop (4816) is over, and the burst's gems do not rotate while its expansion, lift and scale (5949–5962) still run. Browser (`street-view-browser.mjs`): a `page.mouse` drag turns the eye at 1100×760 and a `touchscreen` drag at 390×667; a tap after a drag does not route; the stick appears only on touch sizes and moves the player; capture is tested in headed Chrome only (a separate opt-in script, not in CI, documented in `docs/first-person-advice/phase0/RESULTS.md`'s harness).

**Exit.** Walk and look together on a real phone (portrait and landscape) and on desktop in Chromium and Firefox; no pan, pinch or zoom leaks; mouse capture never engages without the toggle.

## 3. Phase 3: crosshair and click interaction

**Goal.** Everything a tap can reach, the keyboard and a centre crosshair can reach too, exactly once.

**Work orders.**

1. **One resolver.** `street_view.resolve_target(screen_pos: Vector2 = Vector2.INF) -> Dictionary` returns `{kind, id, anchor: Vector3, action: Callable}` or empty, and is the single source for the focus ring, the tap and the keyboard. Candidates: neighbours, the mural, the speaker, the two history stops, surprises, eateries (and their open counters), the museum hub, Boeddeker, each with an exterior anchor (the point `tap_screen_distance` already uses) and strict line of sight (`line_of_sight_clear` from the eye to the anchor, with no own-footprint exemption for targets, unlike captions). For the focus ring: within 6 m (`TALK_PROMPT_RADIUS_M` :64) and 20° of the eye's forward, ordered by angle, then distance, then id, so the result is stable. For a tap: the existing screen-distance reach, interactables resolved before the ground. The resolver preserves the special dispatch for Celeste (`talk_with_celeste`) and Rook (`check_sound_with_rook`) and never re-selects a nearer neighbour than the one addressed.
2. **Focus ring and activation.** The ring (a `Control` circle plus a one-line caption, "Bea · Talk (Enter)") follows `resolve_target()` each tick. Enter and T call `street_view.activate_focus()`, which re-resolves before acting (a target that moved out of reach or sight is dropped silently) and dispatches only through existing gameplay methods: `talk_to_nearby_neighbor` 6360 / `walk_to_neighbor` 8412, `walk_to_mural`, `walk_to_speaker`, `walk_to_history_stop`, `inspect_neighborhood_surprise` 5690, `activate_eatery`, `inspect_tenderloin_museum_hub` 8718, `inspect_boeddeker_park` 4584. T today talks to the nearest neighbour; in street view it prefers the focus target. Map-view dispatch (`handle_scene_tap` 14491 with street view off, and `study_test.gd:646–665`) is unchanged.
3. **Ground taps.** Phase 1 already caps the ground ray at 60 m and rejects the horizon. Before any mutation, a ground tap needs a finite positive hit within 60 m whose straight 2D segment from the player is walkable (`segment_walkable`) and whose end passes `is_walkable_point` 8088; otherwise nothing happens and nothing is announced. This deliberately excludes a detour A* could find; the map view keeps its "no walkable point" message.
4. **Behind guards elsewhere.** `player_overlay_rect` 7114 is already empty at eye level because the body is hidden, and `fit_overlay_around_player` 7134, `layout_reward_banner` 5873 and `trigger_feel_good_burst` 5908 contain no unprojection themselves (the burst's world position is chosen at 5927). The projections that do need `is_position_behind` → `visible: false` are the activity snapshots: `bea_invitation.gd` `snapshot()` 124 (projection 130), `helper_bench.gd` `snapshot()` 302 (310, 314), `quiet_seats.gd` `snapshot()` 157, `film_easel_snapshot` 3137, `scene_overlay_snapshot` 11785 (11806).

**Tests.** A wall between the eye and a neighbour removes the focus ring; a target behind the camera is never focused; a sky tap and a tap on an unwalkable point do nothing (player position and `current_path` unchanged, no announcement); click, crosshair and keyboard each trigger the same method exactly once (count with a test hook); the ring's screen point is inside `play_area_rect()`. Browser: at 390×667 the ring and caption are visible and 44 px apart from the street button.

**Exit.** Bea, the mural and the speaker are each reachable by crosshair, by tap and by keyboard alone, from street view, without leaving it until the activity itself takes over.

## 4. Phase 4: eye-level UI and conversations in street view

**Goal.** A conversation no longer throws the player back to the map; dialogue, the talk prompt, the marker and the reward banner read at eye level.

**Decisions.** Conversation camera: *no automatic turn; a "Face speaker" key (Shift+T) and button*. Dialogue: *lowered world bubble first, subtitle panel as the fallback when the bubble cannot fit*.

**Work orders.**

1. **Camera-ownership adapter.** Inventory first: only three modules save and restore a camera pose today: `rhythm_circle.gd` (`return_pose`, eight fields, saved at 262, restored at 294–301, plus the selected building's material), `lantern_procession.gd` (292–300) and `voting_story.gd` (235–241). `shared_panel.gd`, `bea_invitation.gd`, `helper_bench.gd`, `quiet_seats.gd`, `loan_homecoming.gd` and `civic_listening.gd` only project, inspect or lay out; they are not restorers and are not migrated. New `godot/camera_owner.gd` (RefCounted, `game` reference): `claim(owner: String) -> int` returns a generation token and records the map pose (Rhythm's eight fields, so nothing is lost) and the street state (`enabled`, `yaw`, `pitch`); `release(token)` restores only if the token is current, resumes at the player's current position, and never re-captures the mouse. A manual camera takeover (pan, rotate, zoom, overview) or a mode flip invalidates pending restores; a stale or repeated release does nothing. Owner transfers (an activity opening another) are explicit `transfer(token, new_owner)` calls; a stack is added only if a real transfer needs it. `panel_camera_pose()` 9221 keeps its own comparison signature and is not folded in. Land the adapter after Phase 3 and before eye-level conversations, map view only first, and prove the full suite unchanged (`study_test.gd` 4117 and 4149–4152 compare Rhythm's restored pose).
2. **Conversations stay at eye level, behind a development gate.** Until the Phase 4 and Phase 5 transition tests pass, eye-level conversations are enabled only by a constant in `street_view.gd` (`EYE_CONVERSATIONS := false`); the player-facing rule is unchanged until then. There is no `talk_partner` term in the rule today (S:48); the change is in `frame_conversation` 6390, which sets `following_player = false` (6397): when the gate is on, street view is active and the partner is within 6 m, keep following, stop any route, record the partner through `street_view.begin_conversation(index)`, preserve the heading (only Face speaker turns), and hand the panel the eye's projection of the partner's face (1.6 m) for its layout; walking again ends the conversation as it does today. Side docks stay allowed; the Info fallback stays complete. The hub ("G", `show_block_panel` 15524) does not detach the camera today, so it must explicitly suspend street view for the hub's ownership and resume on close. `end_conversation` 6475 already calls `follow_player`. Neighbours already face the player (`finish_neighbor_arrival` 8966).
3. **Bubble and prompt.** Speech anchors are chosen at creation per kind (`SPEECH_BUBBLE_HEIGHT_M` :199 is 5.0 for the map; applied at 4758, 5298, 5729 and 8755): neighbour speech and the talk prompt (`update_talk_prompt` 6322) use 2.05 m in street view and keep their map heights otherwise. The fit uses `fit_overlay_around_player` as today; when it cannot fit, the Info fallback (4824–4840) becomes the subtitle panel of decision 4. `speech_bubble_screen_rect` already uses the depth-aware scale. Validate at 390×667 portrait and 667×375 landscape.
4. **Marker, arrow, burst.** `update_guide_marker` 6279: marker at 2.4 m instead of its map height while active; `update_guide_arrow`: done; `trigger_feel_good_burst` 5908: place the burst 2 m in front of the eye when the world position is behind or out of frustum.
5. **Panels.** `show_block_panel` 15524 in talk mode: the side dock on wide layouts stays; on narrow layouts the panel is a bottom sheet over the street (already the compact layout). `refit_panel_camera` stays a no-op at eye level; the panel lays out around the eye's projection of the partner instead.

**Tests.** A bubble for a speaker 1.35 m from the player, at the chosen 2.05 m anchor, sits inside `play_area_rect()` and is not deferred at 1100×760; the adapter restores Rhythm's eight fields and the selected material exactly; a manual takeover during an activity cancels its restore; a repeated release is a no-op; toggling street view off during an activity and closing it leaves the map pose as the map would have it; the arrow is visible with the objective 180° behind; the banner is visible and unclipped at 320×667; a conversation keeps perspective and `talk_partner`; "Face speaker" turns the yaw to the partner within 0.5°; ending the conversation leaves the eye where the player stood; the adapter restores the exact map pose after every activity that used to save its own (compare with today's arrays).

**Exit.** Readable at desktop, narrow portrait and short landscape; a screen-reader walkthrough (bridge announcements only) of meeting Bea passes.

## 6. Phase 6: whole district, art and performance

**Goal.** Lift the one-block limit without breaking the pace, the pck budget or phone frame rates.

**Decisions.** Long walks: *4.8 m/s with a "Skip ahead" fade for routes over 60 m; no 24 m/s or ×2.2 at eye level*. Art: *stylized; facades stay labelled unverified*. Scope: *replace the x ≥ −50 half-plane with a polygon of the walkable district (the `walk_bounds` rect 463 intersected with the street corridor), reviewed by the creator*.

**Work orders.**

1. **Scope.** `in_block()` → `in_scope()`: the polygon above; the corridor to the Black Hawk and the museum included once the Skip-ahead exists.
2. **Pace, then Skip ahead.** First, in `advance_walk` 13854, enforce 4.8 m/s while street view is active (today the corridor speed at 13866–13868 is only avoided because the rule suspends street view west of x = −50; widening the scope removes that accident) and keep the inactive speeds exactly (24 m/s corridor, ×2.2 on web) with tests on both sides. Then "Skip ahead": offered when the remaining polyline length exceeds 60 m; pressing it fades to black (0.3 s, or a cut under reduced motion), and after the fade re-validates the route (stop, replan, suspension or a repeated press cancels that generation), trims it to the final 20 m, moves the player there, updates the camera and the route ribbon, and preserves the arrival intent (neighbour, mural, speaker or history stop). Never speed the eye.
3. **Soft obstacles.** Trees, lamps and counters block manual street movement only: a radius test in `walk_step` 8311 (guarded by `street_view_active()`) against the planar tree points `street_tree_points` 940, lamp positions and `eatery_counter_points`, with sliding; `is_walkable_point` 8088 and A* (`find_path` 8146) untouched.
4. **Eye-level detail.** Only where Phase 0 screenshots show the need: doors on short plinth segments and subtle facade banding in the existing shader, all procedural; no textures.
5. **Rooftops.** `set_rooftop_details_visible` 1661 beyond about 140 m with hysteresis at eye level; the previous visibility is restored on exit (the map's cutaways also toggle it). Cutaways themselves stay off at eye level (`update_occlusion` 14147 clears them deliberately).
6. **Performance.** Measure on the creator's phones first (the Phase 0 build is on the LAN while the desktop serves it). The desktop spends 7.7–8.1 ms of script per frame; the first suspects are `update_tree_sway` 5064 (every canopy transform every frame), `update_people_animation` 3574 and `update_readable_scene_labels` (per-label `active()` calls). Targets: 30 fps sustained on a mid-range Android phone; no regression in the map view.
7. **Pck.** Measure after each change; stop and rethink when the headroom under 2 MiB falls below 200 KB (the rule in section 0).

**Tests.** Scope polygon membership for the opening block, the corridor and a point outside; exact speeds: 4.8 m/s active, 24 m/s inactive in the corridor, ×2.2 inactive on web (the current `study_test.gd` 896 upper bound is not enough); Skip-ahead moves the player onto the route, keeps `following_player` and the arrival intent, and is cancelled by Stop, a replan, a suspension or a repeated press; soft obstacles stop manual movement but not A* routes; rooftop hysteresis never flickers across 30 frames at 140 m and visibility is restored on exit; a frame-time budget test in the browser suite (average under 20 ms at 1100×760 on the CI runner, recorded not asserted at first).

**Exit.** The creator approves frame times on their phones; pck under 2 MiB; the map view unchanged.
