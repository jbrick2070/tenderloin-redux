# Election Walk 2024: sources and game boundaries

The creator requested the most recent San Francisco mayoral election. This episode uses the **November 5, 2024 consolidated general election, including the mayoral contest**, through two entries in the Department of Elections' **October 3, 2024 published polling-place list**. Later relocations have not been verified. The visit is a fictional neighbor's map-making walk through those published records, rather than a reconstruction of actual Election Day operations or current voting guidance.

The [official published list](https://sfelections.org/tools/election_data/datasets/2024-11-05/PODJ011_PollingPlaces_20241003.txt) was read in the browser again during this round. They are **address pins**, not surveyed historical doors, room locations, precinct assignments or proof of actual operation at those locations on Election Day. The source list's precinct fields are not assigned to fictional residents or the player. Faithful Fools, 234 Hyde, and Glide, 330 Ellis, are other retained entries, explicitly outside this two-stop walk. This is a small selection, not a list of every polling place.

`scripts/build-voting-places.mjs` derives `godot/data/voting_places.json` offline from those exact rows, EAS coordinates and retained OSM Eddy centerlines, using the project's uniform meter projection. It records source SHA-256 values. An observation position is the nearest Eddy centerline point displaced toward the corresponding address by 6m (Cadillac) or 8m (Boeddeker). These are **game observation stops outside buildings**, not claimed polling entrances. Native walking/clearance checks remain necessary; they do not establish real physical access. City/OSM source geometry and actual distances are not moved.

The museum hub and stylized park gate are neither the election goals nor polling-room callbacks. No verified shared entrance or room is inferred from a shared building or park name.
