# Playtest and polish brief

Prepared October 23, 2026 following the creator request for additional polish. This is a proposed evaluation brief, not implemented UI or a completed playtest. Finish the active Election Walk verification before changing the tested build or running competing captures.

## First player journey

Start with a fresh isolated browser profile. Record build revision, viewport, input method and settings. Use only visible controls and ordinary keyboard/touch input. Record hesitation as an agent observation; human comprehension and pacing still need human testing.

1. Start exploring and identify the next useful action from the visible screen. Check whether the objective names a person, place or action the player can recognize.
2. Walk toward it, stop midway, inspect the map and resume. Check whether the objective, Stop action and destination stay understandable without taking over a manual camera.
3. Have a conversation. Read every choice, try Back and reopen. Check whether the response acknowledges the chosen action and the next step remains visible.
4. Complete Two Dogs Home, the Lantern Procession and, after verification, Election Walk. For each record the invitation, hands-on choice, world change, ending and return to ordinary exploration. Capture where the result is visible and whether it is understandable at the default view.
5. Repeat critical transitions at 390x667 and 320x667 with touch input. Check readers, target sizes, focus, scroll, accidental movement and whether feedback obscures the play area.
6. Try quiet play, independent sound controls and reduced motion. Reload at an episode boundary and verify honest transient/persistent behavior.

## Candidate polish, ordered by player value

- Quest journey: keep unlocked quests open to switch between. Show the selected goal, completed accomplishments and open invitations without exposing every future quest. Earn new invitations through meaningful completion, resident recognition and visible consequences; reveal them as surprises. Offer Follow this invitation or Keep exploring, keeping the invitation available. Preserve honest visit-only/reset behavior. Keep planned eras distinct from playable chapters; avoid forced linear order or another cramped navigation sibling.
- Episode endings: a specific resident thank-you, a readable visible world payoff and a deliberate Continue action. Respect existing save/reward rules; visit-only activities must remain honestly visit-only.
- Conversation copy: short active choices, resident-specific replies and a concise next step. Preserve quoted historical/source text and original fictional labels.
- Walking guidance: make destination and remaining distance easy to find, retain Stop/resume and manual-camera intent, and avoid decorative markers that imply surveyed entrances.
- Teaser presentation: a consistent title treatment, concise captions and selected camera views that show real player actions and consequences. Use actual verified footage and original/cleared sound.

These are candidates, not a license to alter working controls without evidence. Ground demonstrated blockers first, then choose one coherent feature for the required pre-code idea pass and implementation review.

## Issue and capture record

For each finding retain: build revision; profile provenance; viewport/input; initial state; exact actions; expected and observed behavior; screenshot/clip; whether reproduced; severity; proposed smallest useful fix. Distinguish source proof, automated interaction evidence, agent judgment and unverified human acceptance.

Teaser capture should favor six concrete beats: neighborhood reveal, a conversation choice, mural creation, dog reunion, moving lantern group and a chapter-ending gathering. Include Election Walk only after its complete gate passes. Future historical and space scenes may appear as explicitly labeled planned concepts; they are not gameplay yet.

## Creator-approved progression acceptance

Switch away from an unlocked quest and return through visible controls. Complete a meaningful action and check that recognition and a visible payoff precede a surprising new invitation. Choose Keep exploring and confirm that the invitation remains available. Follow it deliberately and preserve manual camera intent. Verify that the journal does not spoil unrevealed quests and that reload messaging correctly distinguishes persistent progress from visit-only activities. Human feelings of accomplishment and surprise remain subject to human playtesting.
