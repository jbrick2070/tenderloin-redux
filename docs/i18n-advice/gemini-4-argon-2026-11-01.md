## 1. Spanish (`es`)

### 1.1 Variety, Register, and Pronoun System for the Tenderloin

* **Community baseline:** Spanish speakers living and organizing in the Tenderloin (and connected SoMa/Mission service networks such as El/La Para TransLatinas, La Voz Latina, Causa Justa :: Just Cause, and TNDC tenant groups) are predominantly **Mexican and Central American** (Salvadoran, Guatemalan, Honduran, Nicaraguan), alongside US-born bilingual heritage speakers.
* **Recommended variety:** **US / Mexican–Central American neutral Spanish** (`lang="es"`, `Intl` `es-US`), matching [`D4`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/docs/I18N_PLAN.md#L1214).
  * Avoid Peninsular (`vosotros`, `vale`, `ordenador`, `aparcar`, and **never** use `coger` for "take/pick up/grab" — use `tomar`, `elegir`, `recoger`, or `llevar`, as `coger` is vulgar across Mexico and Central America).
  * Avoid written Rioplatense/Central American `voseo` (`mirá`, `tenés`). Even though Salvadoran, Guatemalan, and Honduran neighbors often use `vos` at home, **`tú` (singular) + `ustedes` (plural)** is the universal, natural standard in San Francisco community signage, tenant workshops, and narrative games.
  * Prefer everyday US/Mexican–Central American lexicon: *la cuadra* (block), *la acera* (understood pan-regionally; safer than MX-only *banqueta* in narration, though *banqueta* is understood `[UNCERTAIN — needs native reviewer]`), *el departamento* or *la vivienda / el hogar* (home/apartment, avoiding Spain's *piso*), *boleto/folleto/cartel* as appropriate.
* **Register and pronoun dynamics:**
  * **UI chrome & system notices:** Calm, direct, ungendered. Prefer infinitives or conjugated `tú` verbs that carry no gender morphology (*Explorar la cuadra*, *Hablar con Bea*, *¿Todo listo?*, *Te damos la bienvenida*) rather than gendered participles (*Bienvenido/a*, *¿Estás listo/a?*), and avoid `-e` / `-x` neologisms in general UI/prose per [`D4`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/docs/I18N_PLAN.md#L1214) so screen readers and older immigrant tenants read smoothly.
  * **Neighbors → Player:** Warm, egalitarian **`tú`** from all five neighbors. In Starlight Vee's drag encore ([`NEIGHBORHOOD_SURPRISES.impromptu_drag_show`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L328), *"darling!"*), use an epicene/ungendered affectionate vocative such as ***¡cariño!*** or ***¡corazón!*** (never gendered *querido/querida*).
  * **Character ↔ Character:** Celeste, Dez, Rook, and Thanh are block peers and naturally use **`tú`** with one another. Bea is an older organizer using a rollator ([`NEIGHBORS`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/world.js#L10)): in Mexican and Central American neighborhoods, younger adults often show warmth+respect to an elder woman via **`usted`** (while Bea addresses everyone as **`tú`**), whereas community-organizing circles often use mutual **`tú`**. Keep Bea's own speech warm `tú`; `[UNCERTAIN — needs native reviewer]` whether Thanh and Dez addressing Bea in [`civicConversation`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/civic.js#L50-L56) and [`programmePoster`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/game.js#L593) sounds more authentic to Tenderloin readers with mutual `tú` or respectful `usted`.

## 2. Vietnamese (`vi`)

### 2.1 Variety, Register, and Pronoun System for the Tenderloin

* **Community baseline:** San Francisco's **Little Saigon** sits directly on **Larkin Street between Eddy and O'Farrell** — literally overlapping the game's walkable Larkin/Eddy corridor! The Tenderloin's Vietnamese community (served by SEADC, Au Co Vietnamese Cultural Center, and TNDC) was shaped primarily by **post-1975 South Vietnamese refugees and later family-reunification immigrants**, with strong Southern and overseas (*hải ngoại*) lexical habits.
* **Recommended variety:** **Warm, Southern-inclusive US Diaspora Standard Vietnamese** (`lang="vi"`, `Intl` `vi-VN`).
  * Use standard modern Vietnamese spelling, **strictly avoiding post-1975 mainland bureaucratic/state-security terms** (never *tổ dân phố* [neighborhood ward committee], *hộ khẩu*, *ủy ban*, *tuyên truyền*, *phản động*).
  * Use Southern / Little Saigon culinary and everyday terms that match how Tenderloin shops actually speak: ***trà lài*** (not Northern *chè nhài*) for Thanh's `"hot jasmine tea"` ([`NEIGHBOR_LINES.thanh`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/characters.js#L92)); ***chả giò*** (not *nem rán*) for `"crispy imperial rolls"` ([`EATERY_HOST_LINES.bowl_basil_pho`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L248)); ***đồ chua*** (*củ cải trắng và cà rốt ngâm chua*) and ***ngò*** (or *ngò rí*, rather than *rau mùi*) and ***cà phê sữa đá*** for [`morning_star_banh_mi`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L262); ***muỗng / nĩa*** or neutral phrasing `[UNCERTAIN — needs native reviewer]`.
* **Pronoun and particle matrix (solving the age/gender trap without cold `tôi – bạn` translationese):**
  * **UI chrome, buttons & objectives:** Omit second-person pronouns wherever natural (*Khám phá khu phố*, *Quay lại khu phố*, *Ghi chép thực địa*). Use **`bạn`** only when a subject/possessive is genuinely required (*Lượt chọn của bạn*).

## 3. Arabic (`ar`)

### 3.1 Variety, Register, and the Yemeni / Tenderloin Community Question

* **Who speaks Arabic in the Tenderloin?** The Tenderloin's Arabic-speaking residents include a prominent, multi-generational **Yemeni American** community (families, SRO/apartment tenants, and corner-market/cafe workers along Turk, Eddy, Jones, Ellis, and Leavenworth, alongside groups like the Yemeni Alliance Committee and the Arab Resource and Organizing Center [AROC]), **as well as** Palestinian, Syrian, Egyptian, Jordanian, Iraqi, and Sudanese/North African neighbors. Notably, the game's explicitly Arab character in Cook & Share — **Nura** ([`MEAL_DATA.RECIPES[0]`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L33-L68)) — is a **Syrian Arab refugee** making *kibbeh bil sanieh* (*كبة بالصينية*).
* **Would Tenderloin Arabic speakers (many of them Yemeni) prefer MSA or Yemeni/regional dialect?**
  * **Strong recommendation: Warm, accessible Modern Standard Arabic (*Fuṣḥā Bayḍāʾ / فصحى معاصرة دافئة ومبسّطة*), NOT Yemeni dialect and NOT Levantine/Egyptian colloquial across the game.**
  * **Why MSA wins for Tenderloin Yemeni and pan-Arab players:**
    1. **Diglossic literacy norms:** In Yemeni culture in particular, clear *Fuṣḥā* carries deep warmth, dignity, and literary respect in written storytelling, civic notices, and historical plaques. Conversely, seeing colloquial Yemeni (*Ṣanʿānī*, *Taʿizzī*, or *Yāfiʿī/ʿAdanī* — which differ noticeably even among Tenderloin Yemeni families!) written out in dialogue bubbles from non-Arab characters named Bea, Celeste, Rook, Dez, and Thanh feels incongruous or parodic.
    2. **Internal cast consistency:** Having Syrian Nura speak Yemeni dialect would break her explicit backstory, while having the whole block speak Syrian/Levantine dialect would exclude Yemeni and Egyptian families.
    3. **How to make MSA warm rather than stiff ("White MSA"):** Ban heavy bureaucratic/news-broadcast *Fuṣḥā* — especially `تَمَّ + مصدر` passives (*تَمَّ إرجاع الطاولة* "the returning of the table was executed" → write active *أعدتَ/أعدتِ الطاولة* or *عادت الطاولة*) and rigid legal connectors (*حيث إنه*, *بناءً على*). Use warm pan-Arab hospitality expressions that resonate immediately with Yemeni and Levantine neighbors alike (*أهلاً بك يا جار/جارة*, *تفضّل/تفضّلي*, *على مهلك*, *حيّنا / حارتنا*), and let Nura's kitchen lines use gentle, natural Levantine-compatible *Fuṣḥā* (*أهلاً، أنا نورة... تعال نفرد العجينة معاً*). `[UNCERTAIN — needs native reviewer]` Have an AROC / Tenderloin Yemeni-connected reviewer check everyday household nouns (stool, rollator, crate, awning) so none leans obscurely regional.
* **Form of address (`m` / `f`) & Western digits:**
  * **Western digits (`0–9`, `ar-u-nu-latn`):** Strongly preferred for San Francisco diaspora readers navigating US street numbers (`361 Turk`, `145 Leavenworth`, `80 BPM`, `1966`) and dramatically safer for bidi alignment (`[UNCERTAIN — needs native reviewer]` confirm in the 150-string pilot).
  * **Player address ([`D5`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/docs/I18N_PLAN.md#L1215-L1219)):**
    * Keep UI chrome 100% gender-neutral via verbal nouns (*الملاحظات الميدانية*, *العودة إلى الشارع*, *بدء الاستكشاف*) and 1st-person plural (*لنفتح حفل الشارع*).
    * For the ~463 player-addressed strings, provide masculine (`address.m`) and feminine (`address.f`) variants wherever the unvocalized script or necessary *kasra/fatḥa* differs (imperatives `اختر / اختاري`, `امشِ / امشي`, imperfectives `تريد / تريدين`, adjectives `مستعد / مستعدة`, pronouns `لكَ / لكِ`).
    * **Critical UX recommendation for the Transgender District context:** Label the setting **`صيغة المخاطبة` ("How neighbors address you")** — **never** *"الجنس" ("Sex/Gender")* — and offer it **immediately when the player chooses Arabic on the startup card**, not buried solely inside Settings after Chapter 1 has already gendered them!

### 4.3 Culturally Sensitive Spots and Risks (`th`)

1. **Political & State-Authority Connotations in Civic Content ([`civic.js`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/civic.js), [`voting_story.gd`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L894-L990), [`mayor_pomp_event`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L393-L401)):**
   * In Thai, translating `"gathering"` as *การชุมนุม* evokes political street protests and emergency decrees; use *งานพบปะเพื่อนบ้าน*.
   * Avoid *บันทึกคำให้การ / ร้องเรียน* (formal testimony/complaint) in the Listening Hour so Thai immigrant tenants do not associate it with police/landlord records.
   * In [`mayor_pomp_event`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L397), render `"The Block Mayor"` playfully (*นายกฯ ประจำช่วงตึก* or *ท่านนายกฯ หน้าซอย*), avoiding royal/high-state protocol (*ราชาศัพท์*) or making it sound like an official SF Mayor proclamation.
2. **Trans & Drag Terminology ([`history`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/game.js#L906), [`livingDistrict`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/game.js#L996), [`impromptu_drag_show`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L323)):**
   * While *กะเทย* (*kathoey*) has rich reclaimed history inside Thailand, in historical/educational plaques about 1966 San Francisco ([`history()`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/game.js#L906)) it can read as informal, overly narrow, or derogatory depending on context. Use ***คนข้ามเพศ*** (*transgender people*) and ***ผู้หญิงข้ามเพศ*** (*trans women*) for historical and civic accuracy, and have a queer/trans-affirming Thai reviewer tune Starlight Vee's voice.
   * For [`magic_carpet_chef`](file:///C:/tenderloin-redux/browser-next/visits/text.js#L354) (`"Wild Woman Chef"`), avoid *ผู้หญิงป่าเถื่อน* ("savage/barbaric woman"); use ***เชฟสาวสุดเฟี้ยว*** or ***เชฟสาวจอมซ่า***.
3. **Buddhist Era (`พ.ศ.`) vs. Gregorian Years (`ค.ศ.`) in Historical Text:**
   * Uninstructed Thai translators or LLMs frequently convert years in prose (`1907`, `1961`, `1966`, `2017`, `2024`, `2026`) into Buddhist Era (`พ.ศ. 2450`, `2504`, `2509`, `2560`, `2567`, `2569`).
