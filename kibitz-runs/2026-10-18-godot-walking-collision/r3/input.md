## G2 — Actual-scale walking, building/courtyard collision, and camera sightline cutaway
- `CharacterBody3D` player (`PLAYER_HEIGHT = 1.72` m, `PLAYER_RADIUS = 0.45` m, `WALK_SPEED = 4.8` m/s) starts on the north Turk Street sidewalk (`17.74, -9.33`, `3.37 m` outside `201006.0006089`).
- Every building mesh attaches a `StaticBody3D` with `ConcavePolygonShape3D` faces, and `is_walkable_point` / `walk_step` enforce exact 2D polygon + courtyard-hole collision (`is_point_in_building_polygon`, `min_wall_distance >= 0.45` m, `walk_bounds` margin) with sub-stepped movement (`<= 0.2` m per micro-step) and angled-wall / street-tangent sliding (`TANGENT_DIRS`).
- Camera occlusion cutaway (`update_occlusion`) tests sightline segments from the orthographic camera direction (`+Vector3(180, 180, 180)`) to the player and active neighbor against building polygons/heights, switching occluding buildings to translucent `cutaway_material` (`alpha = 0.28`) while keeping every mesh vertex and AABB unchanged.
